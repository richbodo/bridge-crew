# Roadmap

Goals for later, each waiting for its own plan or issue. What's being built now is in
[`docs/plans/`](plans/).

## Game Studio

From [Bigger games](plans/bigger-games-2026-11-16.md) (issue #9):

- **A Tune panel.** Sliders for the numbers in a game's `CONFIG` block, such as gravity or
  speed, so a student can change one and see what happens, with no model call. The teacher's
  bigger games all keep their numbers in `CONFIG`, which suits it.
- **More libraries on the shelf**, one at a time, when a game needs one: physics engines
  (matter.js for 2D, cannon-es for 3D), the Phaser 2D engine, and Tone.js for sound.
- **Hosting games online**, so a family gets a link as well as a file. Now planned: play links
  on GitHub Pages, in [Games on GitHub](plans/games-on-github-2026-11-16.md).
- **Multiplayer games.**
- **Video in games**, such as a trailer on a title screen.
- **Image-generation services**, for pictures beyond what can be drawn as code. This needs a
  second AI provider, so it needs its own decision on cost, privacy and safety for students.

From [Game Dev Station](plans/game-dev-station-2026-11-22.md), after its steps 1–5:

- **Exploring the galaxy.** Game Dev Station moves through the galaxy like a ship, and each
  place it reaches can bring something new on screen: a game to fix, a challenge, a visiting
  crew. The star map on the deck's wall is where it starts.
- **Galaxy Maps.** [Galaxy Maps](https://galaxymaps.io)
  ([GitHub](https://github.com/tairea/galaxy-maps-v1)) is an open-source learning platform
  themed around exploring the galaxy: a course is a galaxy of star systems and missions, made
  by Captains (teachers) and explored by Navigators (learners), and progress is recorded as xAPI
  statements. Linked to it, a course could chart the station's route, and making a game could
  complete a mission. Galaxy Maps is a hosted service, so its plan needs a decision on how the
  two sign in and what students' data crosses between them.
- **A second computer** joining the station with a code, so two people can play from two
  laptops. The Desktop shortcut answers only on its own laptop, so this needs a real sign-in
  for whoever joins.
- **Themes** beyond the space station, each with its own names, art, sounds and crew persona,
  and the same rules for young people.
