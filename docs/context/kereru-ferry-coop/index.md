# Kererū Ferry Cooperative — pack overview

**Provenance: entirely fictional.** No such cooperative exists. Every name, vessel, route and
number in this pack was invented for testing. Because it is fictional, no model can have seen
it in training: an answer that gets these details right read the pack.

## What the cooperative is

The Kererū Ferry Cooperative (KFC — the members hate the acronym and use "the Coop") runs
three passenger ferries across Waitapu Inlet. It was incorporated in 1994 as a
member-owned cooperative after the private operator, Halstead Marine, withdrew. There are
1,842 member-shareholders; a share costs NZD 60 and carries one vote regardless of holding.

The board has seven seats. Chair: **Marama Whitiora** (since 2021). Deputy chair:
**Desmond Ovalle**. Operations director: **Fenella Kirk-Rāwiri**. The board meets on the
second Thursday of odd-numbered months at the Tautari Road depot.

## The live decision

The board must decide, before the **28 February** capital-plan deadline, whether to
electrify the **Ōtira Point** route by repowering the *Kārearea* with a battery-electric
drivetrain, or to defer and rebuild its existing diesel main engine.

- Repower quote (Sundstrom Marine, Nelson): **NZD 4.31 million**, plus **NZD 890,000** for
  the Ōtira Point charging berth. Delivery 14 months, vessel out of service for 5 of them.
- Diesel rebuild quote: **NZD 612,000**, 6 weeks out of service, buys an estimated 9 years.
- The regional council's Coastal Decarbonisation Fund would cover **38%** of the repower,
  but only if the contract is signed before **28 February** and the vessel is in service by
  the end of the following calendar year.
- The Coop's cash reserve is **NZD 2.05 million**. Its debt covenant with Tairua Mutual caps
  new borrowing at **NZD 3.4 million** and requires a debt-service coverage ratio above 1.25.

See `operations.md` for the fleet, routes and three years of numbers.
