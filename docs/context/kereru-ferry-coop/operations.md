Electricity at the Tautari Road depot is contracted at **NZD 0.1740/kWh** off-peak until
2029. Sundstrom's modelling puts the repowered *Kārearea* at **41 kWh per crossing** and
claims a **NZD 71,000** annual maintenance saving against the diesel.

## The maintenance backlog

Deferred work valued at **NZD 1.27 million** as at 30 June FY-1:

1. *Kārearea* port shaft and stern gland — NZD 210,000 (the vibration fault).
2. Tautari Road pontoon fenders and gangway compliance — NZD 445,000, mandated by the
   harbourmaster's notice of **17 September**, deadline in 3 years.
3. *Tarāpunga* hull plating survey — NZD 305,000, or retire the vessel.
4. Ticketing system replacement — NZD 310,000; the current vendor exits support next June.

## Membership politics

The **Rangi Bay Commuters' Group** (about 300 members, convenor **Hilda Ngatai**) opposes
any capital spend on Ōtira Point, arguing their route carries more trips for less subsidy.
The **Ōtira Point Residents' Association** holds roughly 520 member votes and has signalled
it will run a slate at the next board election if the repower is deferred again. A special
resolution to raise the member levy needs **60%** of votes cast, with a **quorum of 15%** of
members.
